Guideline 1.1: All non-text content will have text alternatives that will serve the same purpose as the non-text content. For example, an image of a puppy will have a text alternative that describes the image, such as "A small, brown puppy is sitting on top of a hay bale."



Guideline 1.2: All time-based media will provide alternatives for audio and video based content. For example, a video or audio clip will have captions or a transcript that will provide the same information as the original content.



Guideline 1.3: Content will be created in a way that it can be presented in a different form without losing information or functionality. For example, any sensory information can be conveyed in different ways, like text, audio, or a visual representation. This ensures that all users, regardless of their abilities, can access the content.



Guideline 1.4: All content can be easily seen or heard by users. For example, text can be easily differentiated from the background or resized for better visibility, and audio can be adjusted for volume or paused.
