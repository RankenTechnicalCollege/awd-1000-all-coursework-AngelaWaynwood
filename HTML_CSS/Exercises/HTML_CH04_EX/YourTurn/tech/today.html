The Technologies of Today

AI has both helped in major advancements in many areas but has also caused a lot of panic due to the way generative AI is being used. AI is not only ChatGPT answering random questions. It is used for a lot of things, such as predictive maintenance in elevators and escalators, to fraud detection, and climate change models.

Lasers were discovered in 1960, and scientists weren’t sure where to even use them. Now they have gone from a solution with no problem to solve to a tool that is used in almost every field. Eye surgeries, industrial cutting, cosmetic treatments, and barcode scanning, lasers can now be found being used in every part of life.

Quantum Computing has allowed us to solve problems that would have taken a classic supercomputer thousands of years, in about 200 seconds. This happened in 2019 by a quantum computer, Google’s Sycamore. It could lead to major improvements and applications in finance, security, an pharmaceuticals.

The Human Genome Project was completed in 2003, which mapped every gene in the human body. This opened new fronts in the improvement of healthcare by helping doctors target genetic diseases. It has greatly improved the way towards personalized treatments that, before, would have seemed to come from a science fiction book.

Global Positioning System or GPS has made navigating anywhere 1 thousand times easier. It has become a major tool in people’s everyday lives. Without GPS, we would still be using paper maps to figure out where we’re trying to go. It has made traveling and vacationing a lot more fun and easier because having GPS means that you have a very detailed map of the world at your fingertips.
