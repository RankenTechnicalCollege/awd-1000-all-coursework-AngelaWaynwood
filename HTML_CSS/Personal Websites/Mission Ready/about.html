Hi, I’m Jennifer Heim.

I’m a Missouri CPA who has spent more than 15 years in public accounting, with much of my career focused on nonprofit organizations.

And somewhere along the way, I figured out which part of the job I love most:

Helping clients solve the mess before it becomes a bigger mess.

I’ve worked with nonprofits and churches through audits, complex accounting questions, internal control problems, compliance requirements, year-end closes, and the inevitable situations that begin with:

“We have a weird question.”

Those are usually my favorite calls.

Why MissionReady?

After years in public accounting, I realized the work I enjoyed most wasn’t issuing the report at the end of an engagement.

It was everything that happened before it.

Helping a client figure out why something didn’t reconcile.

Explaining what an auditor was requesting.

Finding a practical internal control when an organization only had three people in the accounting department.

Untangling an accounting problem that had been sitting there for six months.

Helping an executive director understand what the financial statements were actually saying.

That’s why I created MissionReady Advisory Services.

I wanted to build a practice dedicated to that work.

Specialized by design.

MissionReady isn’t trying to serve every type of business.

My practice is intentionally focused on nonprofit organizations and churches.

Specialization matters.

Nonprofits operate differently from commercial businesses. Restricted resources, grants, contributions, board governance, donor expectations, compliance requirements, and limited administrative staffing create challenges that deserve someone who understands the environment.

You shouldn’t have to explain nonprofit accounting to the person you’ve hired to help with your nonprofit accounting.

Practical beats perfect.

The technically perfect answer isn’t very useful if your organization can’t implement it.

My approach is practical.

I’ll explain what matters, why it matters, what your options are, and what I recommend.

No unnecessary jargon.

No fifty-page solution to a five-minute problem.

And if I don’t know the answer, I’ll tell you that too.

Because good advice starts with being willing to say, “Let me research that.”

Your organization has a mission.

My job is to help make sure the accounting doesn’t get in the way of it.
