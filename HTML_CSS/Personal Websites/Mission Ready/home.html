Practical help from someone who understands nonprofits.

MissionReady Advisory Services provides specialized accounting and advisory services to nonprofit organizations and churches.

Whether you’re preparing for your annual audit, trying to strengthen internal controls, or dealing with an accounting issue that nobody quite knows how to solve, you’ll get practical guidance designed around your organization—not a generic checklist.

How MissionReady Can Help

Audit Preparation

An audit shouldn’t require your entire organization to stop functioning.

MissionReady helps you prepare schedules, organize documentation, address accounting issues, and get your records ready before they reach your auditor.

The goal is simple: fewer surprises, fewer last-minute requests, and a smoother audit.

Internal Controls

Good internal controls aren’t about creating unnecessary paperwork.

They’re about protecting your organization, your employees, your board, and the mission you’re trying to accomplish.

MissionReady can evaluate existing processes, identify weaknesses, and help design practical controls that make sense for your organization’s size and resources.

Nonprofit & Church Consulting

Sometimes you don’t need an ongoing accounting firm.

You just need someone who understands nonprofit accounting to answer the question sitting on your desk.

MissionReady provides consultation for accounting, financial reporting, policies, processes, and other financial matters affecting nonprofits and churches.

You don’t need another auditor.

You need someone who understands what your auditor needs.

MissionReady does not perform audits, reviews, or compilations and does not issue assurance reports or opinions.

Instead, MissionReady works with your organization to help you prepare for those services.

That distinction matters.

Your auditor remains independent. Your organization gets experienced support. And everyone gets a cleaner process.
