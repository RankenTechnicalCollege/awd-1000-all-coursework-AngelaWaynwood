Accounting problems are easier when you have someone who knows where to look.

Nonprofit organizations have unique accounting, reporting, compliance, and governance challenges.

MissionReady Advisory Services provides focused consulting services for nonprofits and churches that need experienced help without hiring another full-time employee or engaging a firm for services they don’t need.

Audit Preparation

Walk into your next audit prepared.

The weeks before an audit can become a scramble for schedules, reconciliations, documentation, contracts, grant information, board minutes, and answers to questions nobody anticipated.

It doesn’t have to work that way.

MissionReady helps your organization prepare before the auditors arrive.

Services may include:

  • Preparing or assisting with audit schedules
  • Reviewing account reconciliations and supporting documentation
  • Organizing requested audit documentation
  • Identifying potential accounting issues before fieldwork
  • Assisting with year-end closing procedures
  • Reviewing prior-year audit adjustments and recommendations
  • Helping staff understand auditor requests
  • Coordinating preparation of client-provided information
  • Assisting management throughout the audit process

MissionReady works for your organization, not the auditing firm.

The objective isn’t to interfere with the audit. It’s to make sure you’re ready for it.

Internal Control Consulting

Controls should protect your organization—not bury it in paperwork.

Small and midsized nonprofits often face an uncomfortable reality: there aren’t enough people to create the textbook version of segregation of duties.

That doesn’t mean good controls are impossible.

MissionReady helps organizations identify risks and develop practical controls appropriate for their actual staffing, operations, and resources.

Services may include:

  • Internal control assessments
  • Segregation-of-duties evaluations
  • Cash receipts and disbursement processes
  • Payroll controls
  • Credit card and purchasing procedures
  • Bank reconciliation processes
  • Board and management oversight controls
  • Policy development
  • Documentation of financial processes
  • Corrective-action planning for audit findings or control deficiencies

The goal isn’t perfection.

It’s a control environment that makes sense, protects the organization, and can actually be followed.

Nonprofit & Church Consulting

Not every accounting problem requires a long-term engagement.

Sometimes there’s a strange transaction.

Sometimes the board asks a question.

Sometimes your accounting system doesn’t match what you think happened.

MissionReady provides ad hoc consulting for nonprofit organizations and churches that need experienced guidance.

If something falls outside MissionReady’s scope, I’ll tell you.

And when appropriate, I’ll help you determine what type of professional you actually need.

What MissionReady Does Not Do

MissionReady Advisory Services is an advisory and consulting practice.

MissionReady does not provide:

  • Bookkeeping
  • Audits
  • Reviews
  • Compilations
  • Attestation services
  • Assurance reports
  • Audit opinions

Keeping those roles separate allows MissionReady to focus on what it does best: helping your organization get ready, solve problems, and build stronger financial processes.
