Tuna Noodle Cassarole

  • Noddles (your choice, preferably twirly but it doesn't really matter)
  • Canned Tuna
  • Cream of Mushroom Soup
  • Shredded Cheese
  • Parmesan Cheese
  • Salt
  • Pepper
  • Garlic Powder
  • Onion Powder
  • Potato Chips
  1. Preheat over to 400° F
  2. Cook noodles to al dente or a little under if you want the noodles harder
  3. Drain pasta and add it to a 9 x 13-inch baking dish
  4. Add the cans of tuna, cream of mushroom soup, and cheese to the dish
  5. Then mix thoroughly and add another thin layer of cheese
  6. Bake for 15 to 20 minutes depending on the level of brown you want on the crust
  7. Remove from oven and then add crushed potato chips on top.
  8. And then serve
  9. Add more chips to your own portion if you would like
