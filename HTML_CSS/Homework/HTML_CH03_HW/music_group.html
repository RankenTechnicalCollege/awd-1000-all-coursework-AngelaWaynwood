Wildchild
It is Alex Warren's newest album/CD and my favorite song from it has to be "CRY WOLF" I love the tone of it and it has a rare, angry sound and beat to it, which is not his normal tone for his songs.
You'll Be Alright, Kid
This album has Alex Warren's most popular songs on it. Those being "Ordinary" and "Eternity". These songs have a hopeful and yet mournful tone to them. They are some of my favorite that have been released.
Bloodline
This is the first Single I ever listened to from Alex Warren. It has an up-beat and strong tone to it. It is about leaving a cycle, even if it is you 'bloodline'.
